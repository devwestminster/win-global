Documents you’ll need to apply

Your counsellor will check every document, explain what is required and help you prepare a complete application with confidence.

Check your eligibility
  1. 01

    Academic transcript

    Your official record of subjects studied and grades achieved.

  2. 02

    Graduation certificate

    Proof that you have completed your current qualification.

  3. 03

    Passport

    A valid passport with sufficient validity for your application and travel.

  4. 04

    CV

    A concise summary of your education, experience and achievements.

  5. 05

    Statement of Purpose (SOP)

    A focused statement explaining your goals, motivation and course choice.

  6. 06

    Academic or professional references

    Letters from lecturers, teachers or employers who can support your application.

  7. 07

    English language test result

    An accepted result, such as IELTS, where required by your chosen course.
